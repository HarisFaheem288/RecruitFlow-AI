# Manual Recruitment Workflow

## Workflow Trigger
A new candidate application is received.

## Inputs
- Candidate resume
- Job description
- Required skills and qualifications

## Steps
1. Open the resume.
2. Read the candidate's skills and experience.
3. Compare evidence against the job requirements.
4. Identify missing or unclear qualifications.
5. Record findings in a spreadsheet.
6. Prepare a candidate review summary.
7. Send the review to the responsible recruiter for approval.

## Tools
- Email inbox
- PDF/DOCX reader
- Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets

## Human Judgment
The recruiter determines whether evidence supports each job requirement and whether further investigation is needed.

## Approval
A human recruiter makes the final shortlist decision.

## Exceptions
- Corrupted or unreadable resumes
- Missing experience information
- Unclear skill descriptions
- Contradictory qualifications
- Duplicate applications

## Main Bottleneck
Repeatedly reading resumes and manually converting information into structured review notes.

## Evidence Status
This is a hypothesized workflow based on a simulated recruiter. Actual timing, error rates, and user feedback have not yet been measured.
